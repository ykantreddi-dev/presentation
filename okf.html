Challenge

Why enterprise knowledge needs more than keyword search

The Challenge

Enterprise knowledge breaks down before AI can help

The source deck frames the problem first: knowledge exists, but it is scattered, inconsistently structured, and difficult to ground reliably in an answer.

  • 1Policies, FAQs, and operational processes are spread across separate files and systems.
  • 2Traditional keyword search often misses the most relevant material when wording differs from the user query.
  • 3Standalone LLMs can sound fluent while still answering incorrectly when domain context is missing.
  • 4The real need is a pipeline that can both find the right knowledge and generate a response grounded in it.

Without retrieval discipline and structured knowledge, AI answers are fast but not dependable.

OKF Framework

OKF turns loose content into structured knowledge assets

The extracted source text points to OKF as the organizing layer: records carry explicit metadata and relationships so retrieval has something reliable to work with.

okf-record/ ├── id # stable identifier for the knowledge item ├── title # readable topic or document name ├── tags # domain labels such as hr, policy, process └── related # connected FAQs, policies, or process documents
Structured metadata
Linked records
Search-ready context

OKF is not the answering system by itself; it is the structure that makes reliable answering possible.

RAG vs OKF

These are complementary layers, not competing ideas

RAG

Retrieves relevant information at query time and uses it to ground response generation.

  • Finds semantically similar and relevant content
  • Supplies context to the model before answering
  • Optimized for answer generation from retrieved evidence
OKF

Organizes knowledge in a structured format with metadata and relationships across records.

  • Stores knowledge with fields like id, title, tags, and related
  • Maintains document relationships and knowledge hygiene
  • Optimized for building and maintaining the knowledge base

Conclusion

What the OKF research is really arguing for

Closing

Key takeaways from the OKF presentation

1

Fragmented knowledge is the root problem

When policies, FAQs, and process documents remain disconnected, both search and AI answers degrade quickly.

2

OKF and RAG solve different parts of the same system

OKF organizes the knowledge base; RAG retrieves the right context and grounds the answer against it.

3

The outcome is faster and more reliable enterprise answers

Structured knowledge plus intelligent retrieval produces context-driven responses that are easier to trust.
